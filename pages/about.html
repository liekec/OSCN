ABOUT OSCN

A bottom-up network, by researchers

OSCN unites researchers, staff and students from across Nijmegen who want to make Open Science the norm — not the exception — in scholarly practice.

Our goals

Reach & engage

Engage scholars with Open Science and create opportunities to learn, connect and exchange knowledge.

Inspire & enable

Inspire and support scholars in exploring and adopting Open Science practices in their work.

Empower & support

Empower scholars to help shape Open Science-related policies, infrastructure and services.
